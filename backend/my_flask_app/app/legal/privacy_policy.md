# AgriVision Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** [effective date]

AgriVision is operated by **[developer / organisation name]** ("we", "us"). If you have a question about this policy or your data, contact us at **[contact email]**.

AgriVision helps farmers and drone operators check crop health, by photographing a plant with a phone or flying a survey with a drone, and plan targeted spraying. This policy explains what information the app handles, why, who else receives it, and how to delete it.

## 1. Information we collect

- **Account details:** your username, email address and password. The password is stored only as a one-way hash, so nobody, including us, can read it. We use these to sign you in and to keep your records separate from everyone else's.
- **Google sign-in:** if you choose "Sign in with Google", Google tells us your name and email address. We never receive your Google password.
- **Profile details you choose to add:** role, organisation, phone number, and a location you type (for example a village or district). All of these are optional and are shown on your profile.
- **Pilot credentials you record:** licence type, number, issuer and dates, so you can keep your drone paperwork with your flights.
- **Drones, missions and surveys:** drones you register, field boundaries and flight paths you plan, survey results, and spray plans. These are the work the app does for you.
- **Photographs and camera frames:** photos you take or choose to analyse, and frames from your drone's camera, together with the results (diagnosis, severity, confidence and suggested treatment). We keep them so you can look back at your history.
- **App preferences:** for example notification settings, so the app remembers your choices.
- **Password-reset codes:** stored only as a hash and valid for 10 minutes, to let you reset a forgotten password.

## 2. Information that stays on your phone

- **Your phone's location** is used only to place a "you are here" pin on the mission map. It is not sent to our servers.
- **Your language and theme choices** are stored on the phone only.

## 3. Who else receives your information

We do not sell your information, show advertising, or include analytics or tracking tools.

- **Google Gemini (the crop advisor):** only when you tap **Know More** or ask the crop advisor a question, the photo, the app's diagnosis and your question are sent to Google's Gemini service to produce an answer. We do not keep a copy of the conversation. Google processes it under its own terms (https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/terms) and, depending on the service tier used, may retain it for a period and use it to improve its services. If you do not want a photo sent to Google, do not use the advisor: every other feature works without it.
- **Reports you send:** if you report an advisor answer, we store the answer, your question, the reason you chose and any note you add, so a person can review it.
- **Google Sign-In,** if you choose to use it.
- **Email delivery:** password-reset emails are sent through [email provider].
- **Hosting:** our servers run on Railway (https://railway.app), which stores the information described in section 1 on our behalf.

We may disclose information if the law requires it.

## 4. Security

Information travels between the app and our hosted servers over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection, and passwords are stored only as hashes.

If you connect the app to a drone ground station on a local field network, for example a laptop at `192.168.x.x`, the traffic between the app and that station uses unencrypted HTTP on that local network, because such a station has no public address and cannot hold a trusted certificate. Use it only on a network you trust.

## 5. How long we keep it

We keep your information until you delete your account. Password-reset codes expire after 10 minutes and are deleted when a new one is issued. [If the database has automatic backups, state here how long a deleted account can remain in them.]

## 6. Deleting your account

You can delete your account and its data at any time:

- **In the app:** Settings, then **Delete account**.
- **On the web:** https://agrivision-production-ae8e.up.railway.app/account/delete

Deleting your account permanently removes your account details, profile, pilot credentials, drones, missions, surveys, spray plans, scans, photographs and camera frames, reports you sent, and preferences. It cannot be undone.

## 7. Children

AgriVision is intended for adult farmers, agronomists and drone operators. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

## 8. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we will update the effective date above and, for significant changes, tell you in the app.
